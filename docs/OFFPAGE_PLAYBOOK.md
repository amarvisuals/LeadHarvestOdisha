# LeadHarvest India – 90-Day Off-Page SEO & Authority Playbook

*(Strictly white-hat: zero paid link schemes, link farms, or automated spam.)*

---

## Days 1–30: Foundation, Verification & Directories
1. **Search Console & Bing**:
   - Verify canonical domain (`https://leadharvest.in` or `leadharvestodisha.vercel.app`) via DNS TXT or `NEXT_PUBLIC_GSC_VERIFICATION`.
   - Submit `https://<domain>/sitemap.xml` and inspect 5 core URLs (`/`, `/pricing`, `/features/no-website-filter`, `/blog`, `/tools/pitch-generator`).
2. **Google Business Profile**:
   - Create **only if** you operate from a verified physical office address with signage; otherwise skip GBP and focus on SaaS directories.
3. **SaaS Directory Submissions**:
   - Product Hunt, G2, Capterra, AlternativeTo, SaaSHub, BetaList, IndieHackers, and Indian startup directories (YourStory/StartupIndia profiles, Crunchbase).
   - Link each profile cleanly to `/` and `/features/no-website-filter`.

## Days 31–60: Digital PR, Free Tools & Video
1. **Link-Earning Free Tools**:
   - Share `/tools/pitch-generator`, `/tools/whatsapp-link-generator`, and `/tools/agency-roi-calculator` in Indian web-designer & freelancer communities (only when genuinely answering a question—never drop unsolicited links).
2. **YouTube Tutorial Series (3–5 min screen recordings)**:
   - *"How to Find 50 Businesses With No Website in 3 Minutes"* → links to `/features/no-website-filter`
   - *"How to Build a Clean Excel Lead List Without Duplicates"* → links to `/blog/build-local-business-lead-list-excel`
   - *"Cold WhatsApp Outreach Script for Indian Web Designers"* → links to `/tools/pitch-generator`
3. **Digital PR Data Study**:
   - Publish an original study: *"State of Small Business Websites in Odisha & Tier-2 India (2026)"* using aggregated, anonymized percentages from demo/public data.

## Days 61–90: Partnerships, Guest Posts & Social
1. **Guest Posts & Podcast/Newsletter Mentions**:
   - Pitch Indian digital marketing blogs, freelance newsletters, and SaaS communities with practical tutorials (never generic AI fluff).
   - **Outreach Template**:
     > Subject: Practical tutorial idea for [Site Name] readers (no fluff)  
     > Hi [Name], I noticed your recent guide on [Topic]. I wrote a step-by-step breakdown on how Indian web designers qualify local businesses without websites using Excel & WhatsApp workflows. Happy to share an exclusive, original 1,500-word draft with real Indian scripts. Interested?
2. **LinkedIn & Instagram Reels**:
   - 2 carousel posts/week showing real "Before/After" pitch frameworks and Opportunity Score breakdowns.
3. **Backlink Tracker Columns**:
   - `Target URL | Linking Domain | DR/Authority | Anchor Text | Status (Pitched/Live) | Date | Notes`.
