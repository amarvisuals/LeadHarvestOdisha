# 6-Month State-Wise Off-Page SEO & Authority Playbook

No paid links, link farms, or automated spam. Focus on genuine data-driven PR, local communities, and legitimate directories.

## 1. Monthly Cadence
- **Months 1–2 (Wave 1 States):** Pitch the Q4 Opportunity Reports (`/reports/odisha`, `/reports/maharashtra`, `/reports/west-bengal`, `/reports/uttar-pradesh`) to regional startup newsletters and local business journalists.
- **Months 3–4 (Wave 2 States):** Partner with college E-Cells (IIT/NIT/state universities) and regional WordPress/Webflow/freelancer meetups in Pune, Bengaluru, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, and Chennai.
- **Months 5–6 (All India):** Submit LeadHarvest India to reputable SaaS/startup directories (Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, SaaSHub, BetaList) and publish 2 short state-wise YouTube/LinkedIn walkthroughs per week linking to `/leads/<state>`.
