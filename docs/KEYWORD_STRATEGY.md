# LeadHarvest India – Keyword Strategy & Topic Clusters

**Positioning:** B2B lead-data platform that finds local businesses across India from public business directories, with smart filters (like *"businesses with no website"*) and one-click Excel export.  
**Rule:** One primary keyword and unique search intent per URL to prevent keyword cannibalisation.

---

## 1. Keyword Map (5 Pillar Clusters + Supporting Content)

### Pillar 1: B2B Lead Generation Software (India)
- **Pillar Page:** `/` (Home) — Primary: `B2B lead generation tool India`
- **Supporting Pages:**
  - `/features/all-india-coverage` — `Indian business database by state and city` (Commercial)
  - `/features/excel-export` — `export business leads to Excel India` (Product)
  - `/blog/b2b-lead-generation-india-2026` — `B2B lead generation strategies India` (Informational)
  - `/blog/lead-generation-tool-vs-buying-database` — `lead generation tool vs buying business database` (Comparison)

### Pillar 5: Local Programmatic Intent (`/leads/<state>/<city>/<category>`)
- **Pattern:** `<Category> leads in <City>` (e.g. `dentist leads in Cuttack`, `restaurant leads in Bhubaneswar`, `gym leads in Odisha`).
- **Rollout:** Quality-gated (indexed only when unique content & data thresholds pass; released in batches starting with Odisha cities × top 20 categories).
