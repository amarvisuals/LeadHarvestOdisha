# LeadHarvest Odisha – User Guide

## Getting Started

### 1. Step 1: Select Odisha Locations
- By default, **all 30 districts and major commercial hubs of Odisha are selected**.
- You can filter with the search bar (e.g. type `Bhubaneswar`, `Cuttack`, `Ganjam`, `Puri`).
- Use **"Select All Odisha"** or **"Clear"** to customize your target geography in one click.

### 2. Step 2: Choose Business Categories
- Select any of the popular pre-defined industries: Restaurants, Hotels, Gyms, Hospitals, Real Estate, Salons, etc.
- **Add custom category**: Type any niche (e.g., `Solar Panel Dealers`, `Makhana Suppliers`) and click **"Add Custom"** or press Enter.
- **Save as Preset**: Save your favorite combination of cities and categories to reuse anytime.

### 3. Step 3: Skip Data You Already Have (Optional)
- Drag and drop your previous Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or CSV lead sheets into the upload area.
- The system automatically audits each file:
  - Total rows detected
  - Valid rows
  - Blank rows ignored
  - Invalid phone numbers flagged
  - Internal duplicates identified
- Automatic column matching connects "Mobile", "Contact No", "Company", "Locality" without manual work. You can also customize mappings via the **"Map Columns"** button.
- All valid entries are added to the **Known Records Index** and excluded from current and future runs.

### 4. Step 4: Click "Start Collecting"
- Check the confirmation card showing cities count, categories count, and historical records to skip.
- Choose between:
  - **Demo / Reliable Engine**: Fast offline generation of authentic Odisha leads for demonstration or testing.
  - **Justdial Live Engine**: Live web scraping via Playwright with anti-block pacing.
- Click **"Start Collecting"**.

### 5. Live Progress Screen
- Watch the animated SVG hourglass, rotating dashed ring, and glowing horizontal progress line.
- Check live counters: **Total Found**, **Duplicates Skipped**, **New Clean Leads**, **Elapsed / Remaining Time**.
- **Pause / Resume**: Need to pause? Click "Pause". Click "Resume" whenever you are ready.
- **Stop & Save Leads**: Stop the job at any time; all leads gathered up to that second will be neatly saved into your Excel file.

### 6. Downloading Your File
- On completion, confetti celebrates the delivery.
- Click **"Download Excel (.xlsx)"** to save your file:
  - **Sheet 1 ("Leads")**: Clean verified leads, styled header, frozen top row, clickable website links.
  - **Sheet 2 ("Summary")**: Search parameters, totals, and execution duration.
  - **Sheet 3 ("Skipped Duplicates")**: Complete audit of records excluded and matching rules applied.
